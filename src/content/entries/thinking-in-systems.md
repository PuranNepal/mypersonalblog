## The idea I am sitting with

When something goes wrong, I tend to look for the nearest event. A systems perspective encourages a slower question: what structure keeps producing this pattern?

That shift changes the kinds of solutions worth considering. A recurring problem may need a different feedback loop rather than another reminder.

## A question for everyday life

Where am I responding to a delay as though it were a failure?

Learning, exercise, and building trust all involve a gap between effort and visible results. Remembering that gap can make it easier to avoid changing course too quickly.
