## Making room

I keep returning to the relationship between an idea and the conditions that make it possible. Time, quiet, and a place to work can seem incidental until they are missing.

## A small takeaway

Rather than waiting for a perfect stretch of uninterrupted time, I am trying to protect one modest block each week for writing. A little room can still be a beginning.
