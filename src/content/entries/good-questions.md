---
title: "Keep a shelf for unfinished questions"
description: "Not every interesting thought needs an immediate answer. Some need somewhere to wait."
date: 2026-10-02
kind: signals
tags: [Questions, Notes]
---
My favorite notes are often questions I could not answer when I wrote them down.

Why does an explanation suddenly click on the third reading? What makes a tool feel calm? When does a useful routine become something we stop seeing?

Keeping these questions somewhere visible is a way of giving curiosity a memory. An answer may arrive later, disguised as a sentence in a book or a conversation over lunch.
