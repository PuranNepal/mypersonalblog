On a walk this morning, I noticed the sound of a gate I have passed a hundred times. Nothing about the gate had changed. I had simply left my headphones at home.

Attention works a little like editing. Removing one layer allows something quieter to become legible.

I am trying a small experiment this week: one walk each day without anything playing. No productivity goal. Just room for something to show up.
